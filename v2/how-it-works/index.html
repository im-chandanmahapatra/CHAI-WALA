How token ordering works

Chaiwala replaces the counter queue with a token on your phone. It takes about thirty seconds from opening the page to paying.

  1. PickChoose from the menu. Tap a drink to set sugar and size right on its card.
  2. PaySlide to pay. Razorpay opens with UPI, cards and wallets. The counter confirms the payment before issuing a token.
  3. CollectYour token updates live. When it says Ready, show the QR code at the counter and pick up your order.

Why the price is always right

The amount you pay is calculated by the counter from its own price list, not by your phone. If an item sells out while you are ordering, you are told before any money moves.

Questions

Do I need an account to order?

No. You add items, type the name to call out and your mobile number, and pay. There is no sign-up or password.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled by Razorpay, so you can pay with UPI, a debit or credit card, or a wallet. Chaiwala never sees your card or UPI PIN.

What is the token?

After payment you get a short code such as A-042 and a QR code on your phone. Show it at the counter and staff scan it to hand over your order.

Can someone else use a screenshot of my token?

No. The QR code works once. The first scan marks the order as collected, and any later scan shows the time it was already collected.

What if I close the page after paying?

Your token is saved on your phone, so reopening the page brings it back. You can also use Find my token with the mobile number you paid with.

How long will my order take?

Before you pay, the order screen shows a live estimate based on how many orders are ahead of you. Your token then updates from Paid to Brewing to Ready.

What are the three eras?

The menu is split into the 1900s adda, the 2000s coffee house and the 2020s café. Each has its own drinks, bites and music, and you can order from any of them.
