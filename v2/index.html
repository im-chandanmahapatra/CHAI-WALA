How it works

Thirty seconds, then chai.

    Slide to pay. Razorpay opens with UPI, cards and wallets, and the counter confirms the payment itself.

Questions

Good to know.

Everything else is in how it works.

Do I need an account to order?

No. You add items, type the name to call out and your mobile number, and pay. There is no sign-up or password.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled by Razorpay, so you can pay with UPI, a debit or credit card, or a wallet. Chaiwala never sees your card or UPI PIN.

What is the token?

After payment you get a short code such as A-042 and a QR code on your phone. Show it at the counter and staff scan it to hand over your order.

Can someone else use a screenshot of my token?

No. The QR code works once. The first scan marks the order as collected, and any later scan shows the time it was already collected.
